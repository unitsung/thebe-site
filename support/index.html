Thebe Support / 技术支持

Questions, bug reports and feedback / 问题、故障反馈与建议

English

Contact. Email unitsung@outlook.com. Replies are usually within a few days.

When you report a problem, please include:

  • Your device and iOS version, and the Thebe version (Settings > About).
  • Which service the problem involves (for example a media server, a container manager, or the AI assistant) and what you did.
  • What you expected and what happened instead. A screenshot helps. Please remove passwords, tokens and API keys from anything you send.

Common questions.

  • Thebe has no content of its own. It connects to services you run yourself, so you need your own services to use it. The developer does not operate any server and cannot see your data.
  • It will not connect to my service. Check that the address and port are reachable from your device, and that you are on the same network or have remote access set up. If you use a self-signed certificate, enable “Allow Self-Signed” in the service's connection settings.
  • The AI assistant does not answer. Thebe uses an AI model provider you configure with your own key. Check the address, key and model name in Settings. Anything that changes something asks for your approval first.
  • Thebe Pro. A one-time purchase handled by Apple. To restore it on another device, use “Restore purchases” on the Thebe Pro screen while signed in with the same Apple Account. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • My data. Settings, accounts and tokens are stored on your device; passwords and tokens are kept in the iOS Keychain. See the Privacy Policy.

中文

联系方式。 发邮件到 unitsung@outlook.com，通常几天内回复。

反馈问题时，请附上：

  • 设备型号、iOS 版本，以及 Thebe 的版本号（设置 > 关于）。
  • 问题涉及哪个服务（例如媒体服务器、容器管理或 AI 助手），以及你做了什么操作。
  • 你预期的结果和实际发生的情况。附上截图会更快定位。请先抹掉截图和文字里的密码、令牌和 API 密钥。

常见问题。

  • Thebe 自己没有任何内容。 它连接的是你自己部署的服务，使用前需要有自己的服务。开发者没有服务器，也看不到你的数据。
  • 连不上我的服务。 先确认你的设备能访问该地址和端口，并且处在同一网络，或已配置好远程访问。如果服务用的是自签名证书，请在该服务的连接设置里打开“允许自签名”。
  • AI 助手没有回复。 Thebe 使用你自己配置的 AI 模型服务商和密钥。请在设置里检查地址、密钥和模型名称。任何会修改内容的操作都会先请你批准。
  • Thebe Pro。 一次性买断，由 Apple 处理。换设备时，用同一个 Apple 账户登录，在 Thebe Pro 页面点“恢复购买”即可。退款由 Apple 处理，见 reportaproblem.apple.com。
  • 我的数据。 设置、账号和令牌保存在你的设备上；密码和令牌放在 iOS 钥匙串。详见隐私政策。
