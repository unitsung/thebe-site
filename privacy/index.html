Thebe Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Last updated / 更新日期: 2026-10-30

English

Thebe is an app that connects to services you run yourself (media servers, download tools, a Thebe Core, and similar) and to AI model providers you choose. The developer does not operate any server that receives your data.

What the developer collects. Nothing. Thebe has no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting service of its own, and the developer cannot see your data.

What stays on your device. Your service addresses, accounts, tokens and app settings are stored on your device. Secrets such as passwords and API tokens are kept in the iOS Keychain. You can back up, export or delete this data in Settings > Data & storage. Deleting the app removes it.

Where your data goes. Only to the places you configure:

  • Your own services (for example a NAS, media server or Thebe Core). Their handling of data is up to you.
  • An AI model provider you choose, when you use AI chat. Your messages, and any content you ask the assistant to read, are sent to that provider and are governed by its privacy policy.
  • Optional integrations you turn on (for example a music or search service) receive the requests needed to do their job.

Purchases. The Thebe Pro purchase is handled by Apple. The developer receives no payment information and no personal details.

Children. Thebe is not directed at children.

Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.

Contact. unitsung@outlook.com

中文

Thebe 是一个连接你自己部署的服务（媒体服务器、下载工具、Thebe Core 等）和你自选的 AI 模型服务商的 App。开发者没有任何接收你数据的服务器。

开发者收集什么。 什么都不收集。Thebe 没有自己的统计、广告、跟踪或崩溃上报服务，开发者看不到你的数据。

留在你设备上的内容。 服务地址、账号、令牌和 App 设置保存在你的设备上；密码和 API 令牌等机密保存在 iOS 钥匙串。你可以在“设置 > 数据与存储”中备份、导出或删除这些数据。删除 App 即清除。

数据会去哪里。 只会发到你自己配置的地方：

  • 你自己的服务（例如 NAS、媒体服务器或 Thebe Core），数据如何处理由你决定。
  • 使用 AI 对话时，你选择的 AI 模型服务商。你的消息以及你让助手读取的内容会发送给它，受它的隐私政策约束。
  • 你主动开启的可选集成（例如音乐或搜索服务）会收到完成其功能所需的请求。

购买。 Thebe Pro 的购买由 Apple 处理。开发者不会收到支付信息或个人资料。

儿童。 Thebe 不面向儿童。

变更。 本政策变更时，新版本会发布在同一地址并更新日期。

联系方式。 unitsung@outlook.com
